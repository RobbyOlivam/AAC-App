# How to use

## Getting started
The app speaks for you. Tap picture buttons to build a message, then tap the message to say it.
- Everything works **offline**. Nothing you say leaves the device.
- Buttons **never move by themselves**, so your hands can learn where words are.
- Tap **Take the tour** (top of this page) for a one-minute walkthrough of the main screen.

## The main screen
- **Message bar** (top): words you tap appear here. Tap it to say the whole message.
- **Speak, delete, clear** (next to the message): say it, remove the last word, or start again.
- **Suggestions** (under the message): likely next words, and a ✨ full-sentence idea from the grammar helper. Nothing is said until you tap one.
- **Core words** (the big board): the words people use most. Their colours show the kind of word (yellow people, green actions, blue describing words…).
- **Folders** (buttons with a little tab): open a page of words, like Food or Animals. **Back to Core** at the top of every page takes you back.
- **Quick strip** (Yes, No, Stop, Help, Break, Hurts): always there, on every page. One tap says it straight away.
- **Menu** (bottom or side): Home, ABC (keyboard), Phrases, Forms, Find and Settings.

## Building messages
- Tap words one after another: "I" "want" "more".
- Tap the message bar or the speak button to say it.
- With **Speak each word when tapped** on (Settings → Voice), each word is also said as you tap it.
- After a word from a folder, the app goes back to Core by itself (turn this off in Settings → Board).

## Word forms (want → wanted)
- Tap **Forms** after a word to change it: "want" → wants, wanting, wanted.
- Tap **Forms** with an empty message, then a word, to pick that word's form.
- You can also **press and hold** a word button to see its forms.

## Keyboard, predictions and abbreviations
- **ABC** opens the keyboard. Predicted words appear above it; tap one to use it.
- **Abbreviations**: set shortcuts in Settings → Voice → Abbreviations (for example "hru" → "how are you"). Type the shortcut and a space.
- **Speak letters and words while typing** (Settings → Voice) reads letters as you type.

## Ready-made phrases and Find
- **Phrases** has whole sentences ("Wait, I'm talking", "I need a break"). They are said straight away.
- **Find** searches for a word and shows the way to it, step by step, with a glowing outline. Great for learning where words live.

## Saying it your way
- **Tone buttons** (turn on in Settings → Voice): say the message as a Question, Excited, Whisper, Loud, Sad or Sarcastic.
- **Show message to partner** (the screen icon) shows the message in big letters, for noisy rooms.
- **Share** sends the message as text to another app.

## Voice settings
Settings → Voice:
- **Speed, Pitch, Volume** and the **Voice** to use (from the phone's installed voices).
- **Voice age**: Adult, Teen or Kid makes any voice sound younger.
- **Pronunciations**: fix how a word or name is said.
- **Private earbud for previews**: with earbuds in, previews and scanning cues play only in the earbud, and real speech comes out of the phone's speaker.
- **Preview on first tap**: the first tap says the word (privately with the earbud option); a second tap uses it.

## Touch settings
Settings → Touch, for hands that need more time or steadiness:
- **Hold time**: ignore quick brushes; a button must be held this long.
- **Select when finger lifts**: choose on release instead of on touch.
- **Ignore repeated taps**: stop accidental double taps.
- **Spaces between buttons count as touches**: no dead gaps between buttons.
- **Slide to select**: keep a finger on the board and slide; a button is chosen after the finger rests on it. Lifting the finger cancels.
- **Vibrate on tap**.

## Switch scanning
For people who use switches instead of touch (Settings → Switch scanning):
- **1 switch (auto)**: rows light up one after another; press to choose a row, then a button.
- **2 switches (step)**: one switch moves, the other chooses.
- Works with Bluetooth or USB switches, or by tapping anywhere on the screen.
- **Speak each row and button**: with the private earbud option, only the person hears the choices (auditory scanning).

## Vocabulary levels and Explore
- **Vocabulary level** (Settings): show just the first words, or more as the person learns. Hidden words keep their place, so nothing moves when you go up a level.
- **Explore** (turn on "Show Explore button"): shows hidden words faded for a while, to try or model them, without changing the level.

## Editing boards
Open **Settings → Edit boards** (the big card at the top). While editing:
- **Tap a button** to change its word, picture, spoken text, colour (kind of word), recording, level, or to hide it.
- **Tap an empty spot** to add a button there.
- **Add a word** (the notepad icon in the editing bar): type a word and the app picks a picture, the kind of word, a folder and a free spot. Change anything, then tap Add.
- **Move…**: pick a button up and tap where it should go.
- **Copy** a button, then tap an empty spot and choose **Paste**.
- **Page options** (sliders icon): rename the page, change its size, or **Duplicate page**.
- **Undo / Redo** are in the editing bar. Tap **Done** when finished.

## Pictures and photos
- Every starter word has a picture from the Mulberry and OpenMoji symbol sets.
- In the button editor, search for a picture, or use **Photo** / **Camera** for your own. New photos open a **crop** screen: drag to move, pinch to zoom.
- **Photo scenes**: in a folder button, choose "New photo scene" to make a photo with tappable spots.
- Settings → Board → **Symbols** switches between the symbol pictures and the phone's emoji.

## Usage log and report
- Turn on **Keep a usage log** (Settings → Usage log) to see progress: words used, new words, average message length and more in **Usage report**.
- A red dot next to the suggestions shows the log is on. The person can tap it to **pause** logging at any time.
- The log stays on the device. **Export CSV** to share it with a therapist; **Clear log** deletes it.

## Backup and sharing
- **Save backup** keeps everything (words, pictures, recordings, settings) in one file; **Restore backup** brings it back.
- **Restore an earlier version** goes back to how the boards were before.
- **Open Board Format** import and export moves boards to and from other AAC apps.
- **Print boards (PDF)** makes a paper copy for when the device isn't available.

## Device and safety
- **Protect settings**: off, hold for 1.5 seconds, or a PIN, so settings can't be changed by accident.
- **Lock to this app** keeps the device in the talker (screen pinning).
- **Keep screen on** and **Show over the lock screen**.
- A **Quick Settings tile** opens the app from the notification shade.

## Privacy
- Speech, words and logs stay on the device. The app has no internet access.
- Backups and exports only go where you save them.
